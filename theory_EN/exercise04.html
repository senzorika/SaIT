Anatomy of a box plot

A box plot summarises a distribution in five numbers and is ideal for comparing several products side by side. It does not require normality.

Charts in layers

In base R graphics we first draw the “canvas” with plot() and then add layers to it – nothing is redrawn:

plot(line1, type = "l", main = "Filling", xlab = "s", ylab = "ml")  # canvas + line 1
lines(line2, col = "lightblue")        # line 2
abline(h = mean(line1), col = "red")   # horizontal line at the mean
grid(nx = 6, ny = NA, col = "gray")    # vertical grid
Financial chartsA candlestick chart (candleChart from quantmod) shows the opening, closing, highest and lowest price for each day. Indicators such as MACD, RSI or Bollinger bands are moving statistics over these data – the same principle as a control chart in production quality control.
Common mistakebarplot(a, b) does not draw two series – the second argument is the bar width. Two series are drawn as a matrix: barplot(rbind(a, b), beside = TRUE).
