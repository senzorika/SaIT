Assessment case studies I

Two practical tasks from the basics of the course: a discrimination test after a supplier change and choosing the best recipe.

very easy A · A new cocoa supplier 3 points

A chocolate factory is considering a cheaper cocoa supplier. The sensory laboratory ran a triangle test: 48 assessors, 21 of them identified the odd sample correctly.

  1. State the null and the alternative hypothesis.
  2. Choose and compute an appropriate test (α = 0.05).
  3. Write one sentence of conclusion for management. Can you claim the chocolates are the same?

HintExercise 5a (binomial test) and exercise 13 (similarity test).

medium B · Four yogurt recipes 7 points

The R&D department prepared 4 yogurt recipes (A – D). Each of 12 assessors tasted all 4 samples in random order and rated overall liking on a 9-point hedonic scale.

  1. Show the data in a suitable plot and describe what it shows. (1 p)
  2. Determine the experimental design (dependent / independent samples) and check the assumptions. (2 p)
  3. Choose and compute a global test. (1 p)
  4. If the difference is significant, find out with a post-hoc test which recipes differ. (2 p)
  5. Recommend one recipe to management and justify it (2 – 3 sentences). (1 p)

HintExercises 4, 5a and 5b.
