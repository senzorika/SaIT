Verification of calculations

A record of the independent recalculation of the numbers quoted in the theory and of the corrections from the 09/2026 review – modelled on the “Verification of sources” appendix of the SAP repository.

1. What was verified and how

The review took place on 30 September 2026 in R 4.5.2. It was prompted by the experience of the sister repository SAP (in Slovak), where a check found wrong tables of critical values, examples and citations. The procedure:

  1. Runnability – all 58 scripts (29 Slovak and 29 English) were run in a clean R session with tools/check_scripts.R; all of them finished without an error.
  2. Recalculation – for every theory page the exercise script was run, followed by the code from all task solutions; the output was compared with the numbers in the text.
  3. Standards and literature – the “Related” sections only use editions of standards and book titles from the verified SAP list; the DOI of Pineau et al. (2009) was checked in Crossref.

4. What was not verified

  • Coordinates and labels in the figures (SVG) were checked only where a figure states a specific computed value.
  • Verbal interpretations and recommended limits (“as a rule of thumb”, “convention”) are judgement, not calculation.
  • Outputs that depend on the network or on the user's choice (Google Sheets in exercise 4, stock data, the user's own text in exercise 11b).
  • Slides – their numbers are computed by R at render time and were not compared with the theory slide by slide.
  • The validity of standards outside the list taken from SAP; when a standard is revised, the entry has to be updated in both repositories.

5. How to repeat the verification

Rscript tools/check_scripts.R                # data from GitHub, as students load them
Rscript tools/check_scripts.R --local-data   # data from the local datasety/ folder

The same check runs automatically on GitHub whenever the scripts change (.github/workflows/check.yml), together with a check of the links between SaIT and SAP. When data or a script change, the numbers in the theory text and in the task solutions have to be recalculated as well.
