EXERCISE 20

Assessment case studies II

Three tasks from the whole course: preferences and JAR, sensory shelf life and a panel audit with an experiment design.

Script (SK): cvicenie20.RScript (EN): exercise20.R

How it works

  • Download the script exercise20.R – it contains the data and tasks of all three studies.
  • Submit your solution as a commented R script with a short written conclusion for each study.
  • 15 points in total: study A 3 points, B 4 points, C 8 points.

easy A · Flavours and sweetness 3 points

A dairy plans a new range of flavoured milk. 120 consumers chose their favourite of three flavours (strawberry 58, peach 41, mango 21). Those who chose strawberry then rated it on a JAR sweetness scale.

  1. Is the flavour preference uniform? Choose and compute a test. (1 p)
  2. Compute the shares of the JAR categories and draw a chart. (1 p)
  3. Recommend whether and how to adjust the sweetness of the strawberry milk. (1 p)

HintExercises 5a (χ² test) and 12 (JAR scale).

medium B · Sensory shelf life of a salad 4 points

20 consumers evaluated a fresh vegetable salad stored at 8 °C every 12 hours, and the time of their first rejection was recorded. Those who did not reject it by the end of the test (72 h) have rejection = 0.

  1. Build the Kaplan-Meier acceptance curve. (1 p)
  2. Determine the median sensory shelf life. (1 p)
  3. The producer wants at most 25% of consumers to reject the product at the time of consumption. What use-by time do you recommend? (2 p)

HintExercise 10.

hard C · Panel audit and a new recipe 8 points

A descriptive panel (10 assessors) rated the bitterness of 5 dark chocolates (P1 – P5) in two replicates on a 10-point scale. Before deciding on a new recipe, management wants to know whether the panel can be trusted.

  1. Evaluate panel performance (product discrimination, agreement, repeatability). (2 p)
  2. Find the assessor who harms the panel the most and justify it. (1 p)
  3. Evaluate the differences between products with a mixed model (assessor = random effect) with and without this assessor. Which products differ? (3 p)
  4. The new recipe P6 should be slightly less bitter than P3; the expected difference is d′ = 0.9. How many assessors are needed for a triangle test with 80% power? Is the tetrad better? (2 p)

HintExercises 14, 15 and 16.
